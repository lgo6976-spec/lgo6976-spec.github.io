Privacy Policy

Repeekly Uploader · Effective November 12, 2026

Repeekly Uploader ("the app") is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Repeekly YouTube channels to upload and schedule the owner's own videos. It is not offered to the public.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. The Google Privacy Policy also applies to data handled through YouTube and Google.

Data the app accesses

  • Channel information (channel ID and channel title) through the youtube.readonly scope, used only to confirm that each video goes to the correct language channel.
  • Video uploads through the youtube.upload scope, used only to upload the owner's own videos with their title, description, tags and scheduled publish time.

The app does not access viewers' personal information, comments, subscribers, analytics, or any other user's account.

How data is stored and shared

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Upload records (video ID, channel ID, scheduled time) are kept on the owner's computer only to avoid duplicate uploads. The owner can delete them at any time.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. After revocation, the stored tokens stop working and are deleted.

YouTube API 서비스

이 앱은 YouTube API 서비스를 사용합니다. 앱을 사용하면 YouTube 서비스 약관에 동의하는 것으로 간주됩니다. YouTube와 Google을 통해 처리되는 정보에는 Google 개인정보처리방침이 적용됩니다.

앱이 접근하는 정보

  • 채널 정보(채널 ID, 채널 이름): youtube.readonly 권한. 영상이 맞는 언어 채널로 가는지 확인하는 데만 씁니다.
