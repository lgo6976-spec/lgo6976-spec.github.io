Repeekly Uploader

A private upload tool for the Repeekly family of YouTube channels.

What it does

Repeekly Uploader is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Repeekly YouTube channels. It uploads finished Shorts videos that the owner produced, together with their titles, descriptions and tags, to the owner's own channels, and sets a scheduled publish time for each language channel.

  • It is not offered to the public, and no other users can sign in to it.
  • It only accesses channels whose owner has signed in with Google and approved access.
  • It uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Privacy

See the Privacy Policy for what data is accessed and how to revoke access.
